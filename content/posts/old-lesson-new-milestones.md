I went birding a few days back. It was a Friday evening. I was in no hurry. Another birder had given me a tip off of a Harlequin Duck sighting in another area of the park.
So I got into my car looking for the duck. The duck was not found that day. But while waiting for it to show itself I noticed a lot of Sea Gulls flying closeby.
I decided to try taking a pciture of the bird in flight. This was a good step in itself. It meant I was still willing to learn after many months of failed attempts. 
The attempts were few and far between. But there were attempts and that is what matters.

But that day was different. Something clicked for me and I was able to take a few shots of different birds in flight. They were good shots. Well framed, bird in frame and unblurred.
I have been birding with this camera for almost a year now. I had tried. Multiple times. But, the art of taking photographs of birds in flight felt put of my reach.
Till I remembered my days first playing racqueball almost 14 years back. I would try running after the ball the first few times I played. With some coaching from M and some practice, 
I was able to figure out that I could predict where the ball would reach based on geometry and physics! And so I did and then I had many games of fun racquetball games after that.

I have not played the game for a few years now, but the old lessons remain. I realized taking pictures of birds in flight is similar. Their flight path maybe be different and it will take some 
learning and intuition to get better at it. But the basics remain the same. I was able to get the photographs of the birds and I was so happy to hit that milestone that day!
