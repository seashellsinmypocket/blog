I recently realized that I don't have a good reward system in place. I don't even have a tasks system in place, but that is a story for another post.
I am doing all these things and althought not disciplined and consistent, I am proud of my varied interests and hobbies. I am proud of the things like to learn.
I am proud of the things I do and plan to do while living this beautiful life. But inspite of all the things I do, I don't pat myself on the back. gasp.

It is like what I do is nothing special. That is the bare minimum as a functioning adult. I thought I didn't need the gold star and the "good job" after each task. 
Other people, strangers, family are not going to give that to me. I need to be my own cheerleader. I need to validate myself and the things I do. Maybe this is a remnant of 
the south asian culture I grew up in. That I need to excel in everything. Maybe it is not my culture but a familial wound that I've carried through the years where I thought
I had to prove my worth. But I don't need to prove my worth. Who am I proving my worth too? To an acquaintance that forget to invite me to their birthday after planning about 
it a month prior? To other memmbers on a Slack community that may not even come talk to me when we meet in person if that opportunity ever arose?
Who am I showing my worth to? are they worthy? Or are they playing the same game but with others who they place on a higher imaginary social ladder?

I used to think rewards were for when we were kids. That now as an adult I can do what I want, buy what I want, eat dessert when I feel like it. Nothing to look forward to, 
nothing to work for. Writing that down, pen to paper, sound boring. No wonder I don't do the tasks I need to do. Why do them? if there are no rewards after? Why do them if 
I can get the reward at any time without any work?

Do I want to be playing that game? I am not a kid anymore and I am soon approaching an age where young is not a synomyn I would use to describe myself. So although I don't want
external validation, won't it be better for myself to create some contraints for myself to make this game more interesting to play. I could argue that I could build up to a mindset 
where I don't need rewards to get stuff done and could have intrinsic motivation. But that is a weak argument for me, considering I have read most self help and habit books available
and still struggle with getting stuff done, still struggle with achieving my goals. 

So I commit to approach each task and goal and complete them for points. And with that reward program in place I will encash points to claim items from my dopamine menu, or
buy another art supply.

Let's go!
